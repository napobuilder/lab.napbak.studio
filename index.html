Common Loudness Questions

What LUFS should I master to for Spotify?

Spotify normalizes playback to -14 LUFS integrated. However, most commercial releases sit between -6 and -10 LUFS. Mastering to -14 LUFS is not required — focus on how your track sounds and let platforms handle normalization.

What is the difference between LUFS and dBTP?

LUFS measures perceived loudness over time (how loud it sounds). dBTP (decibels True Peak) measures the absolute peak signal level. For streaming, keep True Peak at -1.0 dBTP or lower to avoid inter-sample clipping during lossy encoding (AAC, OGG).
